The Seven Levels of Reality

☉ ◇ ✦ ✳ ✦ ◇ ☉
1

Physical Reality

The Material Plane

The densest and most manifest level of existence. Physical reality encompasses all matter, form, and tangible phenomena governed by the laws of physics and natural sciences.

Element: Earth | Domain: Matter & Form | Perception: Five Physical Senses
2

Energetic Reality

The Vital Force Plane

The vibrational essence underlying physical matter. This level governs life force, chi, prana, and all energetic manifestations. It sustains and animates the physical body.

Element: Water & Air | Domain: Life Force & Vibration | Perception: Auras & Energy Sensitivity
3

Emotional Reality

The Astral Plane

The realm of emotions, desires, passions, and imagination. This level shapes personal reality through emotional charge and manifests as the astral body. Dreams originate here.

Element: Fire & Water | Domain: Emotion & Desire | Perception: Dreams & Intuition
4

Mental Reality

The Mental Plane

The realm of pure thought, intellect, and abstract ideas. This level governs concepts, logic, consciousness patterns, and the power of mind to shape reality.

Element: Air | Domain: Thought & Reason | Perception: Higher Mind & Contemplation
5

Causal Reality

The Karmic Plane

The realm of causality, karma, and archetypal patterns. This level contains the seeds of all effects and represents the bridge between individual consciousness and universal law.

Element: Ether | Domain: Karma & Causality | Perception: Spiritual Insight
6

Celestial Reality

The Divine Plane

The realm of divine consciousness, spiritual truth, and universal love. This level transcends individual identity and represents pure light, bliss, and unconditional existence.

Element: Light | Domain: Divinity & Truth | Perception: Enlightened Awareness
7

Infinite Reality

The Absolute Plane

The ultimate ground of all existence—the source from which all realities emerge and to which all return. Pure consciousness, oneness, and the eternal void containing infinite potential.

Element: Void & Infinity | Domain: Absolute Being | Perception: Non-Dual Consciousness

✦ As above, so below. As within, so without. ✦

The journey through these seven levels represents the path of spiritual evolution and self-realization.
